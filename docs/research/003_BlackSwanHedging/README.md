Universa Investments, a Miami-based "Black Swan" hedge fund led by Chief Investment Officer Mark Spitznagel, saw its flagship portfolio surge by 4,144% during the first quarter of 2020 amid the COVID-19 pandemic market crash.

How the Strategy Worked

Universa specializes in tail-risk hedging (or portfolio insurance). The mechanics of their multi-thousand percent gain include:
• The "Love to Lose" Model: In normal economic conditions, the fund intentionally accepts small, recurring losses by purchasing deeply out-of-the-money put options. These options act as an insurance policy that loses money when the market rises or stays flat.
• The Black Swan Payoff: When the pandemic triggered a severe and rapid market crash in Q1 2020, those out-of-the-money options skyrocketed in value. Universa locked in its 4,144% returns by cashing in many of its explosive positions while keeping protection in place for further market drops.
• Portfolio Efficiency: Because of the explosive nature of the payoff, institutional clients only needed to allocate a tiny fraction of their assets (e.g., 3.3%) to Universa. This small allocation was enough to completely insulate and effectively turn a traditional S&P 500 portfolio profitable during the 2020 market crash.
