# About Me

I'm Josh — a quantitative researcher and data scientist working at the intersection of **financial markets, optimization, machine learning, and AI**.

My work focuses on turning messy real-world problems into quantitative systems: building optimization frameworks, designing machine learning pipelines, working with alternative and market data, and developing tools that help translate models into actual decisions.

I'm particularly interested in:

- **Optimization & Operations Research** — portfolio construction, allocation, constrained optimization, and decision systems
- **Machine Learning** — statistical learning, anomaly detection, representation learning, and deep learning
- **Financial Markets** — securities finance, market structure, portfolio analytics, and quantitative research
- **AI Engineering** — agents, evaluation frameworks, retrieval systems, and production AI workflows
- **Data & Infrastructure** — scalable research pipelines, feature engineering, APIs, and analytical tooling

## Background

My path into computer science has been somewhat unconventional.

I originally studied **economics and econometrics**, which led me into quantitative finance and investment research. Over time, increasingly technical problems pushed me deeper into programming, optimization, machine learning, and software engineering.

I'm also a **CFA charterholder** and am completing a **Master's in Computer Science at Georgia Tech**, specializing in Machine Learning.

That combination shapes how I approach technical problems: I care about the mathematics, but I care just as much about whether a model survives contact with the real world.
